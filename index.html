Radar Spotters

Storms pop up, grow, and fall apart while the wind pushes them across the map. Watch them on three radar products:

  • REF (1) — how much rain and hail. Green is light, red is heavy, pink and purple means hail.
  • VEL (2) — wind. Green blows toward the radar, red blows away. Bright green right next to bright red = rotation. Tornado alert!
  • ET (3) — echo tops: how tall the storm is. Over 50,000 ft is a monster.

Things to hunt for: hook echoes (the back of a supercell curling around its spin), bow echoes (a line bulging forward — damaging winds!), and thin gust fronts racing out ahead of strong storms. The radar takes a new scan every 4 game minutes.

Warnings! Press ⚠ SVR (or W) for a Severe Thunderstorm warning or 🌪 TOR (T) for a Tornado warning, then press on the storm and drag the way it's moving. Storm reports (hail ◆, wind ■, tornado ▼) pop up as storms do damage. Reports inside your warning score points (more for lead time and a tight box). Reports outside are misses, and a warning with no reports is a false alarm. Warnings only last 20–30 game minutes, so keep up!
